## blader/humanizer

https://github.com/blader/humanizer, MIT, copyright Siqi Chen.

The five entries under "Staging instead of stating" in `skills/roughen/SKILL.md`
(not X but Y, one-line closers, sayings that sound deep, staged run-ups,
arguing with no one) are adapted from sections 1 to 5 of its `SKILL.md`. The
rest of its method, in particular its rewrite step, is not followed here: a
rewrite drifts towards a neutral register and sands off a writer's own voice,
so this skill flags a person's words and only rewrites a model's.

## The vocabulary

The banned words and phrases were compiled from the banned-word lists of ten
public posting skills, keeping only what two or more of them agree on. Lists
of words are not copyrightable expression and none of the ten is reproduced.
